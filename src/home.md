Well met, traveller!
I’m **Kurt Böhm**, a PhD student and research assistant at TU Clausthal’s Institute of Mathematics in the _Scientific Computing_ research group led by Olaf Ippisch with a Master’s degree in Computer Science.

I’m a programmer, computer scientist and tinkerer at heart with a wide range of interests, including high-performance computing, computer graphics (especially image processing), and mathematical optimization.
Some projects I have worked on are listed under [Projects](projects.html) and available [on GitHub](https://github.com/KurtBoehm), and I plan on making some additional projects public in the near future.

My research focuses on [<span class="is-smallcaps">Lineal</span>](https://github.com/KurtBoehm/lineal), an efficient parallel linear algebra library written in modern C++, and C++ libraries connected to it (such as [<span class="is-smallcaps">Θησαυρός</span>](https://github.com/KurtBoehm/thesauros) and [<span class="is-smallcaps">Grex</span>](https://github.com/KurtBoehm/grex)).
A preprint of our first article on <span class="is-smallcaps">Lineal</span> is available [on Research Square](https://dx.doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-6329564/v1).
Like my other publicly available projects, <span class="is-smallcaps">Lineal</span> and the libraries connected to it are available [on GitHub](https://github.com/KurtBoehm).
